## The script | warm
[hello] Hello. This is a small engine for talking presentations.
[script] It starts from a script: chapters, lines, and a cue for each line.
[@wave] A speech model reads the lines, and the music is composed live in your browser.

## The light | inward
[@layers] Every cue tells the light what to become: {word}a word, or {@sphere}a shape.
[@image portrait] It can even show a picture, like this self-portrait made in Blender. <pause 1.2>
[@many] It works in English and in Spanish, with more than one voice.
